# Lore: the mesh network

After the Great Shutdown, the shared internet was gone. What remained was the
mesh: old terminals, industrial controllers and abandoned gateways linked by
short-range radio. Operators call themselves quiet ones.

Deep in the mesh lives the Swarm, an autonomous bot that takes over nodes,
changes keys and spreads viruses. The campaign follows the first operator who
decided to work from a hidden log up to a coordinated attack on the whole
Swarm.

Network rules:

- saves belong to the operator and live in `saves/`;
- mods are the operator's tools in `mods/`;
- updates replace only the binary and never touch operator data.

Virtual incidents in the game are part of the story, not instructions for real
systems.
